The official bilingual (English/French) web platform developed for the TUNCIS 2026 international AI conference during a 2-month full-stack developer internship at Horizon University.

### Key Highlights
* **Core Architecture:** Architected and deployed the core web platform and integrated serverless backend functions on Vercel.
* **Dynamic Registration Engine:** Engineered a multi-currency payment and registration system supporting DT and EUR, factoring in in-person and online attendance modes.
* **Database & ORM:** Integrated Neon serverless PostgreSQL managed with Drizzle ORM for efficient relational data modeling.
* **Abstract Submission Pipeline:** Built paper abstract PDF upload handling and automated attendee notification emails.
* **Handover Documentation:** Authored complete technical handover documentation for ongoing conference administration.
